# Customer Segmentation

Customers fall into exactly one of three segments, tracked on
`customers.segment`:

**Consumer** — Individual buyers, the largest segment by customer count
and order volume, but with the lowest average order value. Almost
exclusively served through the `online` and `retail` channels.

**SMB (Small and Medium Business)** — Business customers with moderate
order sizes and recurring purchase patterns. Served by both Sales Reps and
Account Managers.

**Enterprise** — Large accounts with dedicated Account Manager coverage,
the fewest customers by count but the highest average order value. Because
enterprise revenue is concentrated in relatively few accounts, it is also
the most volatile segment month-to-month — a handful of delayed or
cancelled enterprise orders can visibly move total company revenue even
though the customer count involved is small.
