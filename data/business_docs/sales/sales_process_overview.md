# Sales Process Overview

Orders can be placed through three channels: **online** (self-service,
the largest channel), **retail** (in-person, physical locations), and
**phone** (assisted, typically used by SMB and enterprise accounts).

Every order moves through one of four terminal or near-terminal statuses:

- `completed` — the order was fulfilled and paid; counts toward revenue.
- `pending` — the order is still being processed; only recent orders
  (typically the last few days) are found in this state.
- `cancelled` — the customer or the company cancelled before fulfillment.
- `refunded` — the order was fulfilled but later reversed.

Each region has its own sales team made up of **Sales Reps** (front-line,
handle the majority of orders), **Account Managers** (handle SMB and
enterprise relationships), and a **Sales Director** (regional oversight).
Every order is attributed to the employee who owns it via `employee_id`.
