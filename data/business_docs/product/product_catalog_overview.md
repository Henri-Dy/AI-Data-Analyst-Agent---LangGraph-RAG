# Product Catalog Overview

The catalog spans six categories, each with its own subcategories and
typical price band:

- **Electronics** (Laptops, Phones, Audio, Accessories) — $50–$2,000.
  The highest average selling price category and a major revenue driver.
- **Software** (Productivity, Security, Design, Developer Tools) —
  $20–$500. High margin relative to cost.
- **Office Supplies** (Paper, Writing, Storage, Desk Accessories) —
  $2–$100. High volume, low unit price.
- **Furniture** (Desks, Chairs, Storage, Lighting) — $50–$1,500.
- **Apparel** (Outerwear, Footwear, Accessories, Workwear) — $15–$300.
- **Home & Garden** (Kitchen, Decor, Outdoor, Tools) — $10–$400.

Each product has a unique SKU in the format `SKU-#####`. Revenue is
concentrated in a small set of flagship products per category (a long-tail
distribution is expected and normal, not a data issue).

Approximately 10% of the catalog is marked `is_active = false`
(discontinued). Discontinued products remain queryable for historical
analysis but should be excluded from "current catalog" or "in-stock"
framing.
