# Regional Notes — APAC

APAC is the company's most volatile region. Unlike North America and
Europe, where demand is relatively stable month to month, APAC revenue —
particularly from enterprise accounts — can swing significantly within a
single month due to regional procurement cycles, holiday calendars that
differ from Western markets, and a smaller base of large accounts whose
individual order timing has an outsized effect on the regional total.

When APAC revenue drops sharply in a given month, the first things to
check are: (1) whether the drop is concentrated in the enterprise segment,
(2) whether it is broad-based across products or concentrated in a few
SKUs, and (3) whether the prior month was unusually strong (in which case
part of the "drop" is really reversion after a strong month, not a new
problem).
