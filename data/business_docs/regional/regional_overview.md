# Regional Overview

The company sells through six regions, each with its own customers,
employees, and order history:

- **North America** — the largest region by revenue.
- **Europe** — second largest, a mature and stable market.
- **APAC** — a fast-growing but more volatile region; regional demand can
  shift quickly, especially in the enterprise segment.
- **Latin America** — smaller but steadily growing.
- **Middle East & Africa** — the newest and smallest region.
- **Oceania** — small, stable, low volatility.

Revenue share is not evenly distributed: North America and Europe together
typically account for roughly 60% of total revenue, with the remaining
four regions splitting the rest.
